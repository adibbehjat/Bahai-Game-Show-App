# Birth of Bahá'u'lláh: 02 Recognition of the Báb, Síyáh-Chál, exiles

## Candidates
1. in pack, medium, single: age about 27 on accepting the Báb. BP, WP, BS.
2. in pack, easy, single: Mullá Ḥusayn brought the message. BP, WP. Distractors Quddús, Ṭáhirih (BP: He helped Ṭáhirih), Mírzá Yaḥyá.
3. in pack, easy, TF false: accepted a government post (He declined). BP, WP, BS.
4. in pack, easy, single: Síyáh-Chál year 1852. BP, WP, BS. BS: "Black Pit"; WP/BP about four months. WP lead says exile at 33.
5. in pack, easy, multi: Síyáh-Chál true statements (dungeon in Ṭihrán, chains, vision; NOT over a year). BP, WP, BS.
6. in pack, easy, TF false: freed and allowed to stay in Persia (banished 1853). BP, WP.
7. in pack, easy, multi: exile places Baghdád, Constantinople, Adrianople (Ṭihrán wrong). BP, WP, BO. ʻAkká deliberately left out of the list.
8. in pack, easy, TF true: about forty years of imprisonment and exile. BO, BS.
9. in pack, easy, order: Ṭihrán, Baghdád, Constantinople, Adrianople, ʻAkká. BP, WP.
10. in pack, easy, order: born, marriage, learns of Báb, Badasht, Síyáh-Chál. BP, WP.
11. alt, medium, TF true: He took the name Bahá at the Badasht gathering in summer 1848. BP, WP. DISAGREEMENT: attendees 81 (BP) vs 84 (WP); not used.
12. alt, medium, TF true: He lived two years in Kurdistan as "Darvish Muhammad-i-Irani" (April 1854 to 1856). BP, WP (WP: return 19 March 1856).
13. alt, hard, single: Who was Shaykh Ismáʻíl, for whom He wrote the Four Valleys? BP.
15. alt, hard, TF true: Edward Granville Browne is a Cambridge orientalist (BP).
16. alt, medium, single: Who was arrested heading toward Shaykh Ṭabarsí (1848)? BP.
17. alt, medium, TF true: He was bastinadoed in Ṭihrán for aiding Ṭáhirih. BP.

## Disagreements flagged
- Leaving Baghdád for Kurdistan: both BP and WP give 10 April 1854. WP return 19 March 1856; BP "two years".
- Constantinople arrival 16 August 1863 (WP); BP "about four months in 1863". Departure from Garden 3 May (WP).
- Adrianople arrival 12 December 1863 (WP) vs "December 1863" (BP).
