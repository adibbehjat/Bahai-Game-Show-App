# Birth of Bahá'u'lláh: 04 Later years, Ascension, Shrine

## Candidates
1. in pack, medium, single: arrived ʻAkká 1868 (31 Aug). BP, WP.
2. in pack, hard, single: Edward Granville Browne interviewed Him 1890. BP, WP. Thomas Chaplin (distractor) visited April 1871 per WP.
3. in pack, easy, multi: Mazraʻih (1877-79) and Bahjí (1879-92). BP, WP.
4. in pack, easy, multi: true of Bahjí. BP, SH.
5. in pack, easy, spell Bahjí. BP, SH.
6. in pack, medium, order: ʻAkká, Mazraʻih, Bahjí, Browne interview, passing. BP, WP.
7. in pack, easy, single: passed away 1892 (29 May, aged 74). BP, WP, BS. SH says "before dawn" at the Mansion.
8. in pack, easy, TF false: passed away at Mazraʻih. BP, WP.
9. in pack, easy, single: Will names ʻAbdu'l-Bahá. BP, WP, BS.
10. in pack, easy, TF true: Shrine is the Qiblih. SH, WP.
11. in pack, easy, TF true: gardens around the Shrine. SH.
12. in pack, easy, TF false: buried inside the Mansion (neighbouring house of Sayyid ʻAlí Afnán). SH.
13. removed (owner), hard, order: Mansion completed 1870, move 1879, burial 1892, restoration after 1929, Haram-i-Aqdas from 1952. SH.
14. in pack, medium, single (replaces #13 at the same position): Which Russian Czar received a Tablet from Bahá'u'lláh? Alexander II. Sources: Wikipedia — Bahá'u'lláh (lists Czar Alexander II among those addressed); Bahaipedia — Writings of Bahá'u'lláh (lists Lawḥ-i-Malik-i-Rús, Tablet to Czar Alexander II). Both explicitly mention it.
14. alt, medium, single: Mírzá Mihdí died falling through a skylight (June 1870, aged 22). BP, WP (BP: sacrifice opened the prison gates).
15. alt, hard, single: Mansion builder ʻUdi Khammar, a Maronite Christian from ʻAkká. SH.
16. alt, medium, TF true: Shrine added to UNESCO World Heritage 2008. WP. Only WP.
17. alt, easy, TF true: Shrine is the holiest place on earth for Bahá'ís. BS, SH.
18. alt, medium, single: Báb named Qiblih "Him Whom God shall make manifest" (SH).
19. alt, hard, single: Haram-i-Aqdas approx 13,000 m2 (SH).
20. alt, medium, TF false: Bahá'u'lláh was sentenced to perpetual imprisonment in ʻAkká in July 1868 (WP: royal decree); true as stated, use as TF true.
