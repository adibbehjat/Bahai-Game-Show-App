# Birth of Bahá'u'lláh: 01 Birth and family

Source codes: BO = bahai.org/bahaullah (thin page: meaning of name, 40 years of exile, Tolstoy); BP = Bahaipedia Bahá'u'lláh; BPF = Bahaipedia Family; BPW = Bahaipedia Writings; WP = Wikipedia Bahá'u'lláh; BS = Brilliant Star; SH = Wikipedia Shrine.

## Candidates (in pack = Q#; alt = spare)
1. in pack, easy, single: Birth city Ṭihrán. BP, WP, BS. Distractors Shíráz, Iṣfahán, Tabríz are not given as birthplace in any source.
2. in pack, easy, TF true: born 1817 (12 Nov 1817). BP, WP. bahai.org page gives no date.
3. in pack, easy, TF true: name means "Glory of God". BO, WP.
4. in pack, easy, single: Father Mírzá Buzurg. BP body, BPF, WP. DISAGREEMENT: BP infobox says father is Mírzá ʻAbbás-i-Núrí; WP says "Mírzá ʻAbbás Núrí (known as Mírzá Buzurg)". Same person; "Mírzá Buzurg" used. Distractors: Yaḥyá and Músá brothers (BPF), Mihdí son.
5. in pack, easy, single: Birth name Mírzá Ḥusayn-ʻAlí. BP, WP. Distractors: the Báb's name, Mullá Ḥusayn, son Mírzá Muḥammad-ʻAlí.
6. in pack, medium, single: Mother Khadíjih Khánum. BP, BPF. Distractors Ásíyih (wife), Fáṭimih (wife), Bahíyyih (daughter).
7. in pack, easy, single: nickname "Father of the Poor". BP, WP, BS. Distractors invented parallel nicknames, none in sources.
8. in pack, easy, multi: young man (declined post, generous, accepted Báb; NOT governor of Burujird, which was His father). BP, WP, BS.
9. in pack, medium, multi: wives Ásíyih, Mahd-i-ʻUlyá, Gawhar (not Khadíjih mother, Sárih sister). BPF, BP. Note Mahd-i-ʻUlyá = Fáṭimih Khánum, so Fáṭimih was kept out of this choice list. Gawhar's marriage date is uncertain (early 1860s or 1870s per BPF).
10. in pack, easy, multi: children ʻAbdu'l-Bahá, Bahíyyih, Mírzá Mihdí (not brothers Yaḥyá, Músá). BPF, BP.
11. in pack, easy, TF false: Yaḥyá was a son (he was half-brother). BPF, WP.
12. alt, easy, TF true: Bahá'u'lláh was born into a noble family. BS, BP.
13. alt, medium, single: which wife was mother of ʻAbdu'l-Bahá? Ásíyih Khánum (the Navváb). BP, BPF. Sources differ: BP says Navváb "first wife (1835-1886)" and Ásíyih entitled "Most Exalted Leaf"; BPF says Navváb is Ásíyih's title.
14. alt, medium, TF true: Ásíyih married at 15 when He was about 18 (1835, Ṭihrán). WP; BP says "almost 18".
15. alt, hard, single: Mírzá Músá relationship (brother). BPF.
16. alt, medium, single: Which Sháh's vizier Ḥájí Mírzá Áqásí offered Him a post (He declined). BP, WP.

## Disagreements flagged
- Number of children: BP says fourteen (seven reached adulthood); WP says eleven. Not used.
- Subḥ-i-Azal (Yaḥyá) described as half-brother about 13 years younger (WP); BPF lists him as father's child. Consistent.
